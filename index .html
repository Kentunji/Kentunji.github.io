About

I started on carrier IP networks at Huawei, keeping national mobile networks running for operators in Ghana and South Africa. An outage there meant millions of people offline, and it taught me to care about how systems fail.

Now I work on both sides of that. At AzeniQ I run Kubernetes platforms on AWS: version upgrades on a production EKS cluster, an Istio service mesh with strict mutual TLS, and OPA and Kyverno policies. At PurpleIris I test a SIEM against real attack scenarios and write threat research.

I'm finishing an M.Sc. in Security and Network Engineering at Innopolis University on a full scholarship, and I'm sitting the AWS Solutions Architect and Security Specialty exams in early 2027.
